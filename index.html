Moulana Imtiaz Ali Jafari Al-Najafi completed his rigorous classical studies in the holy city of Najaf Ashraf, Iraq. With over a decade of teaching experience across the globe, Moulana founded Sada e Najaf Online Institute to connect Shia Muslim children and adults directly with authentic Quranic recitation, Ja'fari Fiqh, and Ahlulbayt (a.s) teachings from home.

"Our goal is not merely teaching words on a page, but instilling reverence for the words of Allah (s.w.t.) and love for the Ahlulbayt (a.s.) in the tender hearts of our children living worldwide."
